# Logo sources

All logo files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, where they are marked
**public domain** (mostly because simple text/geometric logos are below the
threshold of originality, or because the copyright has expired).
They remain **trademarks** of their respective owners.

The PNGs in this folder were rendered from the files in `source/`
(`rsvg-convert -w 2000 file.svg -o file.png`) and trimmed to their content.
The 2020 Intel logo was recoloured to a single Intel blue (`#0068B5`).
